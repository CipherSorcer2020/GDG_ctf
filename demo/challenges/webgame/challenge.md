# Acme Corp: Your First Hack

This challenge is not a file - it is a **game**. Open the tutorial game (the link at the
top of this page, or go to `/game`) and work through eight guided levels. Each level is
a little puzzle that comes with its own hints, and the game explains what you learned
as you go.

Solve every level, assemble your flag and press **Claim** - your points land on the
scoreboard automatically.

New to all this? Perfect. That is who the game is for.
